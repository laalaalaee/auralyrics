Quick tour
Auralyrics lives in the tray next to the clock; closing this window keeps it running.
Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free open lyrics database. Only the song title, artist and length are sent.
The glow listens to your PC's sound output to find the beat, never the microphone.
Update checks ask GitHub for the latest version number, nothing more.
It rests while your PC is locked or asleep.
